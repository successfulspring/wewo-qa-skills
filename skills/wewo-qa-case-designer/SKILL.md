# Wewo QA Case Designer

Default sequence: read all requirements → explain the business and deliver a standalone clarification workbook → obtain overall confirmation and consolidated answers → design autonomously → deliver the case draft together with the updated clarification workbook → incorporate batch answers and finalize. The clarification workbook starts before cases and persists throughout. Do not turn every uncertainty, test-design choice or artifact format into another approval.

Use [structured authoring](references/efficient-authoring.md) for native state writing, real-dialogue bookkeeping, selected-unit inspection and staged delivery. Author business facts, derivations, steps, oracles and actual review notes; let the tool maintain IDs, reciprocal references, copied views, fingerprints and formatting. Do not reimplement those supported operations in disposable programs. Compact authored scenario tables and small data constructors may assemble repeated business input; do not require hand-writing every repeated JSON field. Read references by stage, inspect only relevant schema definitions, and keep large state dumps out of the conversation. Complete initial source reading and semantic reviews remain mandatory.

If a usable design baseline already exists, follow [source updates](references/source-updates.md): reconcile Excel edits, read new material, compare with confirmed rules, update affected cases and deliver changes with grouped questions. Reuse prior confirmations; do not restart the initial workflow because another document arrived. Product material alone can start design. Read the [handoff guide](references/handoff-guide.md) only when upstream contributors need content guidance; do not require a fixed template or a development document.

Keep Skill instructions and examples in English. Select delivery language from explicit preference, then sustained conversation language, then source language, defaulting to Chinese. Apply [delivery language](references/artifact-contract.md#delivery-language) consistently; preserve original quotations, identifiers and literal test values.

## 1. Read and explain the whole business

Resolve the bundled [runtime](../runtime-tool.md) and [private state layout](references/artifact-contract.md). Read every authorized document, image, table, prototype and link before asking business questions. Inventory authority, versions and unread content using [source intake](references/source-intake.md). Inaccessible material needs a usable export, authorized viewing or an explicit scope exclusion. Consolidate access problems while continuing to read available material; never invent contents.

For new work, select a dedicated delivery directory (default `qa-artifacts/<feature>/`) before creating the first clarification workbook. Put its context under that directory's `.qa-state/` and reuse the same directory through case delivery. Preserve original inputs at their supplied locations; do not start in the input directory and later move the clarification file merely to satisfy the delivery validator.

Preserve anchored source text, extract each clause into requirement_items and build [design context](references/design-context.md): objects, roles, ownership, states, flows, relationships and rules. Document headings are source anchors, not the business explanation. Product source, database internals and a running implementation are not design prerequisites. Examples supply techniques, never undocumented project facts.

Separate business requirements from implementation reports at clause level. Supplied development documents may explain observable contracts and preparation, but an implementation gap does not change a confirmed oracle. Reuse actual applicable approvals; source role, AI authorship and a newer date alone never authorize replacing requirements. Consolidate genuine conflicts about correct behavior; preserve known expectations when only the reported implementation differs.

Present one plain-language explanation covering who acts, prerequisites, ordered steps, changed state/data, observable results, important formulas with units/rounding/boundaries, exceptions and downstream effects. Include in/out scope and major contradictions or missing rules, with sources and impact. Run the three [requirement audits](references/requirement-readiness.md) and generate the [independent clarification workbook](references/clarification-workbook.md) first using `build-deliverables <root> --stage clarification`. Put every known detailed question in this workbook now, not after case design; no points or cases are required. The user should be able to correct the whole understanding in one reply.

## 2. Confirm understanding once

Use [batched dialogue](references/progressive-dialogue.md). Deliver a clickable `requirement-clarifications.xlsx` link with the business explanation. Ask for overall confirmation/corrections and consolidated answers from this workbook in the same message. Focus this checkpoint on scope, main flows, key rules and major contradictions. Explain consequential contradictions as answerable questions with stable IDs and impact; include local questions with their full details in the workbook, while allowing undecided local items to remain pending for drafting. Do not ask the tester to choose testing techniques, tree depth, IDs, priorities or worksheet organization.

Overall confirmation approves the stated scope and known rules, not unknown expectations. Disclose localized unresolved items and keep them pending; they do not prevent a draft. Only ambiguity that prevents a meaningful agreed scope or main flow blocks the overall baseline. Consolidate such blockers. Reuse applicable confirmation; another message or artifact view is not a reason to ask again.

Read returned clarification cells or chat answers using the [clarification lifecycle](references/clarification-workbook.md#read-answers-and-preserve-evidence). Preserve the displayed summary, question workbook and actual reply with message references and the business snapshot, following [confirmation records](references/requirement-readiness.md). One real reply may both confirm the summary and answer its questions. Explicit corrections update affected facts through scoped amendments. A new overall summary is needed only when the reply does not establish an agreed overall scope/flow. Before design, run validate-requirement-readiness with --draft --confirmed. Never fabricate exchanges or approve facts for the user.

Once overall understanding is confirmed, continue through design and draft delivery in that turn. A statement such as "I can generate cases now" or another request to generate/export is not completion. Pending local rules remain visible while known work proceeds.

## 3. Design and keep progressing

Derive applicability, factors, scenarios and independent outcomes from source clauses and confirmed facts **before** leaves/cases, following [test design](references/test-design.md). Apply coherent business-unit batches with `apply-design` and fix their preflight errors before expanding further; finish with the complete cross-unit/source review. Select methods, boundary partitions, priorities and suites autonomously from business risk. Keep one leaf per independent observable outcome; separate distinct inputs/states/action paths. A coherent case may contain several step-bound assertions. Do not impose fixed counts or depths.

For localized uncertainty, add one open_questions entry with source, impact and options. Mark affected rules/outcomes/leaves/assertions status=pending with matching question_refs; omit unknown expected values and executable comparisons. Display “Pending confirmation” in the delivery language. Continue known steps, data, outcomes and other cases. Never invent a business oracle or turn an entire case into a placeholder because one result is unknown. Keep coverage_plan.status=draft until obligations are resolved; drafts do not require freezing.

Write reproducible preparation, data or unique selection criteria, ordered actions, step expectations, observation locations, timing and cleanup. Assess automation per target after coverage design, preserving UI/interface evidence. Runtime IDs, selectors and sessions belong to Executor. Known comparisons stay concrete; pending business expectations are not runtime conditions.

Perform source-first omission and visible-tree reviews before rendering, including drafts. Review known case paths and disclose unresolved parts. Internal review records are agent work, not extra tester approval. Read [semantic review](references/test-design.md#semantic-review).

## 4. Deliver the draft and updated clarification workbook together

Set points and manifest stage=draft. Use `build-deliverables <root> --stage draft` to export test-points.xmind and all three test-cases.smoke.xlsx, test-cases.regression.xlsx and test-cases.full.xlsx, update the **same** requirement-clarifications.xlsx with real case links, and validate all five public files in one operation. Read its layout-risk report and inspect representative displayed content. Separate render/validate commands remain available for targeted diagnosis; do not rerun an unchanged complete chain after a successful combined operation. Smoke ⊆ regression ⊆ full, sharing stable case/point/assertion IDs.

Deliver five clickable links, suite counts, a concise change explanation and one invitation to answer the remaining questions in a batch. The independent workbook must contain every concrete question, source, business impact, current affected cases and supported options or the specific missing rule. Do not replace detailed questions in that workbook with category labels, ID ranges or counts. It also retains replies and resolved/excluded rows. Case workbooks show pending status and question IDs, without an embedded question inventory.

Highlight new and still-open decisions in chat; do not ask again about resolved ones. When a full chat list is useful or requested, `render-open-questions <state>/test-manifest.json --draft` provides it. See [question authoring and answers](references/progressive-dialogue.md#draft-questions-and-answers). Partial answers resolve only addressed items. Accumulate new local questions until the next coherent delivery; a real answer may reveal a material conflict, so do not promise an exact reply count.

## 5. Refine and finalize

Reuse the initial confirmation and actual batch answers. Update decisions, scoped amendments and all affected rules, outcomes, points and assertions together. Retain unchanged IDs, superseded history and question provenance. Archive resolved/excluded questions with confirmed resolution references instead of deleting them from the clarification workbook. A business answer authorizes its supported changes; do not ask for separate summary, XMind and Excel approvals. Clarify only a remaining material conflict or genuinely ambiguous answer.

Before finalizing, resolve all included material questions or obtain an explicit scope exclusion with lost coverage disclosed. Remove pending markers only after authoritative resolution; fill concrete expectations/comparisons and reconcile extraction and current unit/decision views. Clear candidate leaf links, freeze the resolved coverage plan, then reattach preserved leaves and renew affected source/tree/case reviews. Set points and manifest stage=final. Run final validations and regenerate the same five public files, including the clarification history. No pending business assertion may enter Executor.

Import clarification replies and case-workbook edits before regeneration; reconcile conflicting overlapping views, preserve edits and review changed cases. An expectation proposed in Excel stays pending until business authority is established. Use [artifact contract](references/artifact-contract.md) for commands and legacy migration.

Keep JSON, caches and helpers under .qa-state/ or temporary storage. Do not delete user inputs to clean the delivery root. Inspect exported topic titles and representative normal, rejection, boundary and related-object workbook rows, and validate all readbacks. Final completion requires actual overall confirmation plus necessary scoped answers, closed material expectations, current semantic reviews and five consistent public files. Initial understanding delivery requires the business explanation and independent clarification XLSX, not any case files. Validation establishes consistency, not business truth or exhaustive coverage. Execution is a separate Skill.
